Define Mechanical Engineering?

Technically, mechanical engineering is the application of the principles and problem-solving techniques of engineering for any object, from the design stage to its manufacturing process and the marketplace. Mechanical engineers analyze their work using the principles of motion, energy, and force—ensuring that designs function safely, efficiently, and reliably, all at a competitive cost. Mechanical engineers make a difference. Throughout their careers, they focus on creating technologies to meet human needs and improve human life. Virtually every product or service available in modern life has probably been touched in some way by a mechanical engineer.
